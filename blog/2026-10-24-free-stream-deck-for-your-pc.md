Peyara started as a way to leave the mouse behind. Today the other half arrives: **a free Stream Deck inside the app**, so the phone that moves your cursor can also fire the shortcuts you reach for all day.

<!--truncate-->

## One deck, following your focus

Open the Deck from the touchpad and Peyara looks at the app in focus on your desktop. Photoshop is up? You get layers, brushes and undo. Your browser is up? You get tabs, reload and dev tools. Switch windows and the deck switches with you.

You can also pick an app yourself instead of following focus, and switch decks whenever you like.

## Hundreds of presets, all open

The app ships with hundreds of ready-made presets for design tools, editors, browsers and media apps. Every preset lives in the open [peyara-actions](https://github.com/ayonshafiul/peyara-actions) repository, so adding your favourite app is a pull request away.

## Make it yours

Presets are a starting point, not a cage:

- Search actions for the current app
- Multi-select several actions at once
- Choose how many columns your deck uses
- Drag keys into the order that matches your muscle memory
- Import your own selection and keep it on device

No account, no cloud sync, no subscription.

## Nothing new to install on your PC

The Deck runs through the same tiny Rust server that already handles the touchpad, keyboard, media and clipboard. If Peyara is connected, the Deck is ready.
